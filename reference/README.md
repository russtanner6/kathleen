### `kathleen-book-cover-mockups.jpg`
Two book covers in the "Love Sophia Joy" series by Kathleen Citrine:
- *A Book of Love Poems with Mother Earth Laguna*
- *Homecoming of Hearts with Mother Earth Laguna*

Sent 2026-11-16 with the note: *"Here are 2 book cover mock ups that give a sense of esthetic & energy… The colors aren't coming out as accurate as they are in person & the lighting in here now isn't great but hopefully you get the gist."*

Not a website layout — a palette / mood reference. Observed palette:
- Soft peach / coral (upper gradient)
- Warm 14k-ish gold
- Pale cream / sand
- Light teal / soft blue-green (lower gradient)
- Dark emerald text

Same palette family as the book covers. The handwritten script suggests she values warmth and a hand-crafted feel over perfect polish.
