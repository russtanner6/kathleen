# For Kathleen — Editing Your Website

A one-page guide. No tech jargon. If anything here doesn't work, tell Russ and he'll fix it.

---

## Where to log in

Go to: **https://lovesophiajoy.com/admin** (or the Vercel preview URL Russ sent you)

Log in with the Tina Cloud account Russ set up for you. You'll land on an editor that looks like a visual sidebar next to a preview of your site.

---

## Making edits

Every page of the site is a small list of **sections** you can click into.

1. On the left, click **Homepage** (or the page you want to edit)
2. Click a section — e.g., **Welcome**, or **Foundation**
3. Change the text in any field. The preview on the right updates as you type.
4. When you're happy, click **Save** at the top right.
5. Your change is live on the site in about **1–2 minutes**.

---

## The four things you'll do most often

### 1. Change some text
Click the section → click in the field → type → **Save**. Done.

### 2. Swap a photo
Click the section → click the image field → **Upload** → pick the new one from your computer → **Save**.

### 3. Add a new blog post
Click **Blog Posts** on the left → **+ New Post** → fill in the title, date, excerpt, and body → **Save**.

### 4. Change a gold quote
Those are inside **Homepage → Welcome** (for "Your presence is your present.") and **Homepage → Foundation** (for the two Law-of-Love quotes). Edit the text just like any other field — the metallic gold styling is automatic.

## A few friendly reminders

- **Nothing is ever truly lost.** Every edit is tracked in Git. If you delete something by accident, Russ can bring it back in a minute.
- **Don't worry about breaking the site.** The fields in Tina only let you change *content* — the design and structure are locked down separately in code.
- **If a save seems stuck**, give it 2 minutes, then refresh the live site with Cmd+Shift+R. If still nothing, ping Russ.
- **The site builds in about 1–2 minutes** after you save. Not instant, but close.
